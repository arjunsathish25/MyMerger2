About Daily Activity

This chart visualizes the volume of campaign activities (Sent, Failed, Replied) over the last 7 days. It helps you identify trends, monitor campaign consistency, and see the immediate impact of your outreach efforts on a day-by-day basis.

Mono Mail History

About Mono Mail

Mono Mail refers to one-off emails sent manually from the dashboards, outside of any automated or bulk campaign. This chart tracks the performance of these individual outreach attempts.

Initial Mono Mail is the first one-off email sent to a contact, while Follow-up Mono Mail is a subsequent manual follow-up.

Follow-up History

About Follow-up History

This chart tracks the performance of all follow-up emails, including those sent via the automated sequence and those sent manually from the "Follow-up" page. It helps you understand the effectiveness of your subsequent messages in re-engaging contacts.

Reply Rate Over Time

About Reply Rate

This chart shows the trend of your daily reply rate over the last 30 days. The reply rate is calculated as (Replied Emails / Sent Emails) * 100 for each day.

It is a key indicator of your email content's effectiveness and your audience's engagement level. A rising trend is a positive sign, while a dip might suggest a need to revise your templates or targeting.

About Daily Sent Mails

This chart shows the total number of emails sent per day, including initial outreach, mono mails, and all follow-ups. It provides a simple count of your daily sending volume.
